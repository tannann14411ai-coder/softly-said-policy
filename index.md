# Softly Said Publisher

Softly Said Publisher is an internal tool used only by the operator of Softly Said, a channel of short, quiet videos about feelings, on Facebook, on YouTube ([@softly.said.quotes](https://www.youtube.com/@softly.said.quotes)), and on Pinterest ([softlysaidquotes](https://www.pinterest.com/softlysaidquotes/)).

The tool publishes one short video a day to Softly Said's own Facebook Page, YouTube channel, and Pinterest account, and reads performance data for those videos. It is not offered to the public, and people cannot sign in to it or connect their own accounts.
