# Softly Said Publisher

Softly Said Publisher is an internal tool used only by the operator of Softly Said, a channel of short, quiet videos about feelings, on Facebook and on YouTube ([@softly.said.quotes](https://www.youtube.com/@softly.said.quotes)).

The tool publishes one short video a day to Softly Said's own Facebook Page and YouTube channel, and reads performance data for those videos. It is not offered to the public, and people cannot sign in to it or connect their own accounts.
